# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Real-estate agents managing inbound leads, property context, and follow-up conversations. Prospective buyers and other leads communicate with those agents through email and SMS.

## Product Purpose

Help agents keep opportunities moving by bringing lead management, conversation history, and property context into one simple workspace.

## Positioning

Keystone combines real-estate lead management with AI-powered, context-aware replies and unified SMS and email, emphasizing simplicity and automation.

## Operating Context

Agents review incoming inquiries, work with lead and property details, and respond across email and SMS. Prospective users can explore a simulated buyer-and-agent conversation and request access.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep the interface intuitive and protect user data.
- Agents must approve AI-generated messages before they are sent. Existing automatic outreach code must be reviewed against this requirement before real-world use.
- The interactive demo generates replies with AI using sample listing and calendar data. Email, message delivery, and appointments are simulated.
- Outlook integration and production deployment remain incomplete or unverified; do not present them as validated capabilities.

## Brand Commitments

The product is named Keystone CRM.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains an interactive simulated demo and local recordings in `output/playwright/`. Do not invent customer testimonials, results, or production claims.

## Product Principles

- Make lead follow-up easy to understand and manage.
- Keep the conversation and relevant real-estate context together.
- Give agents control over AI-generated messages before sending.
- Treat user and lead data with care.
